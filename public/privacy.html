STEAM CLUB

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

STEAM Club is the member app of the St. Edward Association of Men ("the club", "we"). This page explains what information the app collects, who can see it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because we are a volunteer-run parish club, not a data business: we do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, and the email address and/or phone number you sign in with. If you use a password, it is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo, and your children’s names and school grades if you choose to add them (see “Children” below).
  • Membership records kept by the club: member number, date you joined, dues status, and points.
  • Activity in the app: volunteer shifts you sign up for, meetings you check in to (and the points earned), and your reminder preference.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, technical information about the crash (device model, app version, error details) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it. It is not used for advertising.

The app uses your camera only to take a profile photo or scan a meeting check-in code, and your photo library only when you choose a picture. It does not read your contacts. “Add to Contacts” only saves a member’s details to your address book when you tap it. Event reminders are scheduled on your own device.

Who can see it

  • Approved club members can see the member directory: names, photos, phone numbers, email addresses, children’s names and grades, and badges such as “Dues Paid”.
  • Club admins can see everything above, including pending requests, and can edit membership records.
  • People who are not approved members cannot see the directory, news, or events.
  • If you share a news post or event outside the app, only what appears on the flyer or message you send is shared.

Who processes it for us

The app runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Crashlytics). Google processes this information on our behalf to run the service and, for phone sign-in, to send you a one-time text message. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Children

The app is for adult members and is not directed to children. Parents and guardians may choose to add their own children’s first names and grades so the club can organize school events and volunteer needs. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. You can remove a child from your profile at any time (Profile → Edit), or ask us to delete it.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while you are a member. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photo, membership record, points and check-in history, volunteer shift sign-ups, and login. If you cannot use the app, email us and we will delete your account for you.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, phone number, photo, and children in Profile → Edit.
  • Turn event reminders on or off in Profile → Notifications.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, using the contact details below.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase and protected by access rules so members can only see what the club allows. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell members in the app.
