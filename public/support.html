Common questions

I signed up but can’t get in.
New accounts wait for a club admin to approve them. The app updates by itself once you are approved. If it has been more than a few days, contact us.

I didn’t get my text message code.
Check the phone number, wait a minute, then tap “Resend”. You can also sign in with an email and password instead.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password?” and we will email you a reset link.

My points, dues, or member number look wrong.
These are kept by club admins. Contact us and we will correct them.

How do I change my phone number, photo, or children?
Profile → Edit.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete Account. This is permanent. If you cannot get into the app, email us and we will do it for you.
